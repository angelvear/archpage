Philosophy & Design

"Space is not a void to be filled, but a medium to be shaped by light and movement."

This residential project explores the intersection of brutalist honesty and ethereal lightness. By utilizing raw concrete masses juxtaposed with expansive glass membranes, the structure creates a dialogue between the permanence of the earth and the transience of atmosphere.

The interior layout follows a rhythmic sequence of compressions and expansions, guiding the inhabitant through a series of sensory experiences where the boundary between interior and exterior is deliberately blurred.
